description=Geologic cross-sections: LIDAR ground profile with projected explorations, exported at exact horizontal and vertical scales.
about=Draw or pick a section line, choose a DEM and (optionally) one or more exploration point layers, each drawn in black, gray or the colours of the layer's own QGIS style, filter by type, set a tolerance, and export the section to PDF, PNG or SVG at exact, independent horizontal and vertical scales with a major and minor grid (green, gray or your own). Add other raster surfaces (water levels, MODFLOW layers) in your own colours, MODFLOW 6 model surfaces and heads, well screens with measured and at-time-of-drilling water levels, and other features: points such as seeps, springs or wells without a log drawn as hydrogeologic symbols, line layers such as creeks marked where the section crosses them, and polygon layers such as a project site or a wetland bracketed where the section runs through them. Long sections split across Letter or Tabloid sheets with match lines.

changelog=
    0.3.6 Other features (Other points before; your rows open unchanged): rows of point, line and polygon layers. Points take a symbol from a new grouped list with pictures: springs, a stream crossing, ponds and wetlands, wells without a log (domestic, public supply, monitoring, pumping, artesian, injection, decommissioned and more), gauges, water-quality and sample sites, outcrops, landslide scarps, survey benchmarks and the general shapes; choosing one sets its usual color, a spring's tail points downhill and the old star still draws on old rows. Line layers such as creeks get the symbol wherever the section line crosses them. Polygon layers such as a project site, a wetland or a parcel get a labelled bracket over each stretch the section runs through, in the frame above the ground and the labels or in rows above the frame, stacked when they overlap, with an open arrow and (continues) where an area goes on past A or A', optional dashed edge lines to the ground or full height, and their own color and solid or dashed line; the legend lists them. A tidier panel: six sections (Depths and Screens and water are now parts of Explorations), headings you can tell apart, layer rows as small cards that join their section when open, tabs Layer, Depths and Water in each exploration layer, shorter names with the explanations in the tooltips, set-once settings under round buttons, the grid spacing under Sheet style and the lowest and highest elevations shown under Manual. Verbose in the ⋯ menu (off by default, remembered for you) shows the hints and readouts under the controls. A problem (values left out, a layer gone, DEM gaps) is one amber line on its row with a count badge; fields guessed from their names show a check badge; a click on a badge opens a card with the details and buttons (Open Depths, Open Water, Looks right, which accepts the guessed fields for good). A status line says Ready or shows the warning. The test runner now ends a worker that never finishes.
    0.3.5 Page orientation: Scale & sheet has Orientation Auto, Landscape or Portrait. Auto works as before (each sheet landscape when the section fits, else portrait); Landscape or Portrait puts every sheet that way, tiled sheets too, and when nothing fits the message names it. A custom page size is sized to the drawing, so Orientation is off for it. Label offsets now give the direction each exploration was projected onto the line (a well north of the line reads S); other points and the direction column of Export points CSV follow. Screens are now reads Elevations | Depths, in the same order as Levels are. In elevation range now sits beside Total depth, and the box once called Water-level table is now Water levels from a table (CSV, Excel...), so it is not read as the water table. Number boxes take text fields too: a CSV added to QGIS, whose columns are all text (like a Boring Summary table), can be the table of water levels and give the tops, depths, screens and levels of a layer. A text that is a plain number (642.3, 1e3, 1,234.5) is read; anything else (NA, dry, 642.3 ft) is left out and listed in the panel, once per field for the table. Guesses still prefer number fields, and now ignore punctuation and a unit in parentheses, so GW Elev. (ft) is found as the water level (DTW when levels are depths). A text column is guessed only when it holds numbers, and the table's Water level box stays empty when no name fits (choose it).
    0.3.4 A compact readout strip: one row of smaller, equal tiles that fits a 700 px dock; the first tile reads where you are (760 ft from A, the elevation and its depth), the ground tile says Ground and a water level shows its date. Exploration colors come from QGIS: Drawn in is now Black, Gray or Color for each layer, and Color takes each exploration's color from the layer's own style (one symbol, categories, ranges or rules), with names, ticks and TD in black; the legend names the colors as the layer's legend does, and the sheet follows when you change the symbology. Data lines By type and the plugin's own type colors are gone (projects that used them switch to Color). Sheet style: set the depth line widths (black and gray, color), and choose a Green, Gray or Custom grid (color, width and solid, dashed or dotted major and minor lines).
    0.3.3 Several exploration layers: Explorations is now a list of point layers (your own explorations, explorations by others, supply wells), each with its own fields, types, water levels, legend name and look (Black like your own, Gray, or a colour; the legend names the layers only when they look different). Fields are guessed from the field names when a layer is added, and dates stored as numbers are shown as dates. Everything chosen is plotted; the new Plotted explorations list shows them by layer, and any you untick are left off that section line (the sheet, the points CSV and the map) and remembered with the project. A layer can stay out of the elevation range (supply wells start that way): its deeper wells stop at the frame with a small arrow and their TD. Other points: point layers such as seeps or springs, drawn as a symbol with a label (a bold blue 8-pronged asterisk by default) resting on the ground line, or at the LIDAR or a field elevation. Hovering the preview names the layer when more than one is on, shows other points too, and says when two explorations share a spot (+1 here). Projects from 0.3.2 open with their layer as the first row and look as before.
    0.3.2 The readout row above the preview is now a strip of equal tiles that never change width or move, whatever you point at, each with a label, the value and a quiet detail line. A popup next to the mouse shows the same values in a small table (or an exploration's details). New Readout row and Popup buttons turn each on or off; both start on and are remembered between sessions.
    0.3.1 Preview tools: hover the preview for the distance and the elevation and depth (bgs / ags) of the ground and every surface, in a row above the plot whose values stay in place; point at an exploration for its top, depth, screen and water levels. A dot follows along the section line on the map, and hovering the map near the line moves the guide on the preview. Scroll to zoom the whole preview (labels and all) up to eight times, drag to pan, double-click to fit (the matplotlib toolbar is gone). Surfaces CSV… exports the ground and surfaces as distance and elevation, one row per point or one column per surface every 10 ft.
    0.3.0 MODFLOW 6 surfaces: + Add MODFLOW 6… reads a model from its run folder (DISV, DIS or DISU; the binary grid file or the text input; no flopy needed) and draws the model top, layer bottoms, the heads of a chosen layer at a chosen stress period (or time step), and the water table (head of the uppermost wet layer), each restyled like any surface, smooth or flat across each cell. Water levels without a leader (ATD, or no screen) now sit just left of the exploration line.
    0.2.0 A cleaner, compact panel: sections in drawing order, all closed when the panel opens, each with a one-line summary in its header. Other surfaces: any number of rasters (water levels, MODFLOW layers) drawn as lines in your own colour, style and width, smooth or flat across each model cell, with optional water-table marks; each can stay out of the elevation range. Screens and water levels: a bracket for the screen, the measured level as an open blue triangle joined to the middle of the screen, at-time-of-drilling (ATD) levels as filled blue triangles, several per exploration from a water-level table, as elevations or depths below the top, with optional elevation and date labels. A legend lists the surfaces and symbols on the sheet. The points CSV lists screens and water levels. Fixed an occasional crash when a layer the panel uses is removed from the project. Projects from 0.1.x keep their settings.
    0.1.2 Compact layout and fill-below are now the defaults: a new project gets the compact layout (title and notes in the margins) with the grid filled below the section in whole major intervals, for more room to draw. Projects saved with 0.1.1 keep their look; projects from 0.1.0 get the new defaults. A manual elevation range is drawn as typed (never filled). All four layout options (compact layout, printer edge, notes, fill the sheet with grid) are under Sheet style.
    0.1.1 Option to hide the bend lines (Sheet style > Draw bend lines). Removed the red 1-inch check bar and print note from the sheet (measure the grid instead). Licensed under GPL-2.0 (LICENSE file). Sheet layout options for more room to draw (Sheet style): compact layout (one-line title, end letters inline, short notes beside the scale bar), printer edge 0.5 or 0.3 in, notes on/off, and fill the sheet with grid below or above and below (replaces "Fill the rest of the sheet with grid below"; older projects keep their choice). A long title line no longer runs off the sheet or over the end letters, and a wide vertical-scale label ("1,000 ft") stays inside the printer edge.
    0.1.0 First release: LIDAR ground profile, explorations projected onto the section (tops from the table or LIDAR, bottoms from a bottom elevation or top minus total depth, optional TD labels; explorations optional), exact-scale PDF/PNG/SVG export with separate horizontal and vertical scales, grids, and Letter/Tabloid tiling.
